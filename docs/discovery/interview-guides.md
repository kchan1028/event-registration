# Discovery interview guides

Use these guides to learn how registration works today and where it costs volunteer time. Each interview is 30 to 60 minutes. Ask about what happened at a recent event, not what people think they would want: "Tell me about the last time..." beats "Would you like...".

Do not show the app during discovery interviews. Showing it turns the conversation toward the solution before the problem is clear. The app belongs in the [Phase 1 usability test](../usability/README.md).

## Consent script

"Thanks for making time. We're trying to understand how race registration and check-in work today, and where they cost volunteers time or cause mistakes. There are no right answers, and nothing you say commits the club to anything."

Ask, and record the answers:

1. "Is it OK if I take notes?"
2. "Is it OK to record audio? It's only for my notes and gets deleted after I write them up." (Optional. A no is fine.)
3. "Your name won't appear in anything we share; we use a code like D03. You can skip any question or stop at any time. OK to go ahead?"

If the person shares a file (a setup sheet, an export), say: "Please remove rider names, contact details, birth dates, and license numbers before sending it, or I'll do it before anyone else sees it."

## Notes format

For each interview, record:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Code and role | D02, registration lead |
| Date and length | 2026-11-20, 45 minutes |
| Events they worked | Two-day weekend, 2025 and 2026 |
| Top three time costs (their words) | "Fixing people in the wrong category on race morning" |
| Numbers they gave | "About 6 hours the week before; 20 to 30 emails" |
| Workarounds | Spreadsheet of field counts kept by hand |
| Quotes worth keeping | One or two, anonymized |
| Follow-ups | Ask for last year's exception list |

Put estimated numbers into [workload-baseline.md](workload-baseline.md) marked "estimate", and workflow steps into [workflow-map.md](workflow-map.md).

## Race director

Goal: how the weekend is configured, what decisions they make, and what they would stop doing if they could.

1. Walk me through setting up the most recent event, from deciding the schedule to opening registration. What did you do first?
2. Where did the schedule and categories come from? Did you copy last year? What changed?
3. How did you set field limits? Were any fields shared between categories? How did you enforce that?
4. How did you set prices, discounts, and the two-day option? Was anything hard to express in the current system?
5. What changed after registration opened (schedule, categories, prices)? How did you handle riders already entered?
6. What went wrong on race weekend that traces back to registration?
7. How many hours did you spend on registration-related work, before and during the event? How confident is that number?
8. If you stepped down next year, what would your successor need that they don't have today?
9. What do you like about the current system that a replacement must keep?
10. What would make you unwilling to switch?

## Registration lead

Goal: the exception workload, where it comes from, and how long it takes.

1. Tell me about the last registration problem you solved. How did you hear about it, and what did you do?
2. What kinds of problems come up most? (Wrong category, license issues, junior waivers, refunds, transfers, duplicates, full fields.) Roughly how many of each per event?
3. Where do you track them? A spreadsheet, email, the registration system?
4. How do you handle a rider whose license can't be confirmed? Who decides?
5. How do you produce the start lists for the timer? How long does it take, and how often is it redone?
6. Walk me through race-morning check-in. What does a volunteer look at? What sends a rider to you?
7. How do you assign bibs? What goes wrong?
8. How many emails, texts, or calls do riders send you around an event? What are the common questions?
9. What would you hand off first if you could?
10. Can you share an anonymized exception list and category setup from a recent event?

## Treasurer

Goal: how money is reconciled and what makes it hard.

1. How do registration payments reach the club? How long does it take?
2. Walk me through reconciling the last event. What did you compare against what?
3. How are refunds and transfers handled? Who approves them?
4. What fees does the club pay today, and who absorbs them? (Note the date and source of any fee figures.)
5. Have there been disputes or chargebacks? How were they resolved?
6. What reports do you need for the club, the federation, or taxes?
7. How many hours does reconciliation take per event?
8. Would the club accept a different payment provider or payout schedule? What would it need to see first?
9. Can you share an anonymized reconciliation example?

## Timer

Optional workflow discovery. Use the [timer export review packet](../timer-export-review.md) for format questions only if timing interoperability is selected for future development.

1. What do you receive from registration, when, and in what format?
2. What do you have to fix or retype before race day? How long does it take?
3. What changes reach you on race morning, and how?
4. What identifiers do you rely on (bib, license number, name)? What breaks when they are wrong?
5. Who uploads results to road-results.com and USAC for these events? What file do they use?
6. Would one export that feeds both your timing software and the results upload save you work?

## Riders and parents (at least five)

Goal: what is confusing or slow about entering and checking in. Include at least one beginner (Cat 5 or one-day license), one masters racer, one parent of a junior, and one rider who has raced both days of a weekend.

1. Tell me about the last time you registered for a road race or criterium. How did it go?
2. How did you decide which category to enter? Were you ever unsure?
3. Have you entered both days of a weekend or more than one race in a day? How was that?
4. Was the total price clear before you paid? Any surprises?
5. (Parents) What did you have to do for your junior's entry and waiver?
6. Have you ever had to change, transfer, or cancel an entry? What happened?
7. Tell me about your last race-morning check-in. How long did it take? What slowed it down?
8. Have you ever contacted the organizer about registration? About what?
9. What one thing would you change?
