# Tulsa Tough

Status: Research case; proposed configuration only. Reviewed November 14, 2026. Edition studied: 2026.

## Published evidence

The [organizer's weekend schedule](https://www.tulsatough.com/blue-dome-criterium) lists three criteriums on June 5-7, 2026. Friday has a narrower program including Men's Cat III, Masters 40+ A, Men's I/II, Women's Pro I/II, and Men's Pro I. Saturday and Sunday add novice, other amateur, and junior fields. Women's II/III and Masters 40+ share a published time on Saturday and a combined row on Sunday.

The [event-guide landing page](https://www.tulsatough.com/event-guide) identifies the 2026 guide but its current navigation advertises 2027. Some linked page titles/URLs are mismatched; use the visible dated schedule body as the cited evidence and confirm the approved guide before resolving details.

## Proposed organizer setup

| Step | Configure | Review boundary |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A three-day criterium program with separate venues | Each day is its own race and actual schedule |
| 2 | Select editable elite, masters, novice, women's, and junior presets | Record explicit allowed license categories and age constraints; do not use a category-number ladder |
| 3 | Mark which categories exist Friday, Saturday, and Sunday | A weekend novice option cannot invent a Friday start |
| 4 | Configure shared-start and classification mappings for combined fields | Same-time publication is a review clue, not by itself proof of shared capacity or separate scoring |
| 5 | Offer individual races and any organizer-approved package components | Two-day and three-day products need explicit contents; no package price inferred from this schedule |
| 6 | Keep kids rides, fondos, and ceremonies in presentation-only schedule items or separate programs | They do not silently create race entries or capacity memberships |

For the paper exercise, select a novice category offered on the weekend and a professional category offered on all three days. The former receives only the two applicable race choices; the latter can have three. Use synthetic rider profiles when checking eligibility and schedule conflicts.

## Reusable lesson

“Three-day weekend” does not mean every category races three times. Reuse the category definitions with a per-day inclusion matrix and separately editable start times. Package composition is derived from approved inclusion rules, not from the event's number of days.

## Acceptance exercise

Copy Saturday's draft to Friday and require explicit removal of unavailable fields. A novice rider must not obtain a Friday entry from a generic “all weekend” button. The combined women's example preserves distinct eligibility and only the organizer-approved scoring/capacity links. See T02 and T09 in the [case index](README.md#proposed-validation-exercises).

## Open questions

Obtain approved caps, race durations, competition classes, combined-field scoring definitions, junior eligibility, available package prices, and which categories participate in which overall competition. This case intentionally does not reconstruct those details from results or import real rider records. Full-event purchase must not be equated automatically with omnium qualification.
